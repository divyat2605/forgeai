# Requester's notes (HR operations lead, Kestrel Shipmanagement)

Used to answer the interviewer's questions in the recorded demo run. In a live run the requester answers at the terminal instead.

- Users: office-based shore staff in Mumbai and Chennai, many in their first year. Seafarers are NOT in scope: they have separate contracts and should be told to contact the Crewing department.
- Tone: friendly, plain English. Give the short answer first, then the details. Mention which policy the answer comes from.
- Never give legal or tax advice, and never discuss any individual's salary or personal records. Send those questions to HR or Payroll.
- Answer only from the policies. If a policy does not cover something, tell the person to contact their HR business partner (HRBP).
- No integrations with the HR portal or payroll system are needed; it only explains the policies.
- Payroll/tax and health insurance would be nice to have, but we do not have those documents ready yet.
- Answers should be short enough to read on a phone.
