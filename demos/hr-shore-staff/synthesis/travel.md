Write the "Business Travel and Expense Policy" (policy number FIN-POL-04, version 2.0) for shore staff of Kestrel Shipmanagement. About 900-1200 words. Sections: Scope, Travel booking and mode of travel, Accommodation, Daily allowance, International travel, Travel advances, Expense claims, Approvals, Personal vehicle use, Ship and port visits.

FACT SHEET (state every fact exactly):
- Domestic trips over 500 km: air travel in economy class. Trips of 500 km or less: train in AC 2-tier.
- Hotel limits per night: INR 6,500 in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Hyderabad); INR 4,000 in all other cities.
- Daily allowance: INR 1,200 per day for domestic travel; USD 60 per day for international travel.
- International travel needs a travel request at least 21 days before departure and approval from the CFO.
- Travel advance: up to 70% of the estimated trip cost.
- Expense claims must be submitted in the KestrelHR portal within 30 days of returning; receipts are required for every item above INR 500.
- Approval of claims: the line manager approves claims up to INR 50,000; claims above INR 50,000 also need the department head.
- Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at INR 12 per km.
- Ship visits: the company issues personal protective equipment (PPE); a port pass is arranged through the port captain at least 2 working days before the visit.
