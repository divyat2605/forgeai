Write the "Leave and Holiday Policy" (policy number HR-POL-01, version 3.2, effective 1 April 2026) for shore-based (office) staff of Kestrel Shipmanagement, a ship management company with offices in Mumbai and Chennai. About 900-1200 words. Sections: Purpose and scope, Annual leave, Sick leave, Parental leave, Bereavement leave, Public holidays and compensatory off, Applying for leave, Leave during probation, Leave encashment on exit, Contacts.

FACT SHEET (state every fact exactly):
- Applies to permanent shore staff; seafarers are covered by their own contracts (contact the Crewing department).
- Annual leave: 24 days per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month.
- Carry forward: maximum 10 days into the next year; carried-forward days must be used by 31 March or they lapse.
- Sick leave: 12 days of paid sick leave per year; a medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive days of sick leave.
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave.
- Paternity leave: 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- Public holidays: 12 public holidays per year; the list for the coming year is published in December.
- Working on a public holiday earns one compensatory day off, which must be taken within 30 days.
- Leave is applied for in the KestrelHR portal; leave longer than 3 days must be applied for at least 14 days in advance.
- Probation: new joiners accrue leave from day one but may only take annual leave after completing 3 months of service.
- Encashment: on leaving the company, unused annual leave is paid out up to a maximum of 30 days, at basic salary.
- Questions: contact your HR business partner (HRBP).
