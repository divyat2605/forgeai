# Leave and Holiday Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-POL-01  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Effective Date:** 1 April 2026  
**Applicability:** Shore-based Office Staff, Mumbai and Chennai Offices  

## Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the framework for leave entitlements and holiday management for shore-based staff at Kestrel Shipmanagement. The primary objective is to ensure that employees are provided with adequate time off to maintain work-life balance, recover from illness, and attend to personal family matters, while ensuring operational continuity across our Mumbai and Chennai offices.

This policy applies strictly to permanent shore staff employed by Kestrel Shipmanagement. It governs the accrual, usage, and administration of various leave types for office-based personnel. Seafarers are covered by their own contracts and are not subject to this specific document; they should contact the Crewing department for details regarding their respective entitlements.

All employees are expected to manage their leave responsibly in consultation with their line managers to ensure that business operations are not adversely affected. This policy serves as a guideline, and specific circumstances may require individual discussion with Human Resources.

## Annual Leave

Permanent shore staff are entitled to 24 days of annual leave per calendar year. This entitlement is accrued at a rate of 2 days per month. Accrual begins from the first day of employment, allowing employees to build up their balance progressively throughout the year.

Employees may utilize their accrued annual leave for vacation, personal travel, or other private matters. It is the responsibility of each employee to monitor their remaining balance via the KestrelHR portal and to plan their time off accordingly. While the company encourages taking full annual leave entitlements to prevent burnout, employees must coordinate with their line managers to ensure adequate coverage within the team.

### Carry Forward Rules

There are specific restrictions regarding the carry forward of unused annual leave. Employees may carry forward a maximum of 10 days into the next year. This cap is strictly enforced to encourage regular time off and prevent excessive accumulation of leave liabilities.

Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March or they lapse. Any balance exceeding the 10-day limit that is not utilized by this deadline will be forfeited. Employees are advised to review their carry-forward status in early February to plan the use of these days before the end of the financial year.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. This leave is intended for periods of illness or injury that prevent the employee from performing their duties. It is designed to support employees during unexpected health issues without immediate financial penalty.

To claim sick leave, employees must notify their line manager as soon as possible on the first day of absence. If the absence extends beyond a single day, continued notification is required until the employee returns to work.

### Medical Certification

A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive days of sick leave. This documentation serves to verify the authenticity of the illness and ensure that leave records are accurate. Employees must submit the medical certificate to the HR department or upload it via the KestrelHR portal within five working days of returning to work. Failure to provide the required certification may result in the absence being treated as unpaid leave or deducted from annual leave entitlements, at the discretion of management.

## Parental Leave

Kestrel Shipmanagement supports employees during significant family events through dedicated parental leave provisions. These policies are designed to support work-life integration and family bonding.

### Maternity Leave

Female employees who give birth are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. This leave is intended to provide sufficient time for recovery and early childcare. Employees wishing to avail themselves of this benefit must inform their line manager and HRBP at least eight weeks prior to the expected due date, if possible, to allow for adequate planning and handover arrangements.

### Paternity Leave

Male employees who become fathers are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave. This leave is available to support the employee during the immediate post-birth period. The leave must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth. Employees should coordinate with their line manager to schedule this leave, ensuring that critical tasks are managed during their absence.

## Bereavement Leave

In the event of a family loss, employees are granted compassionate time off. Kestrel Shipmanagement provides 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member. Immediate family members are defined as spouse, child, parent, or sibling.

This leave is intended to allow employees time to attend funeral services and manage immediate administrative and emotional matters following a loss. Employees should notify their line manager as soon as possible after the event. HRBP support is available for any additional assistance required during this period.

## Public Holidays and Compensatory Off

The company observes 12 public holidays per year. These holidays are recognized across both Mumbai and Chennai offices, subject to local statutory requirements. The list for the coming year is published in December, allowing employees to plan their schedules accordingly. Employees are not required to work on these days unless specifically arranged due to critical business needs, in which case standard compensation procedures apply.

### Working on Public Holidays

If an employee is required to work on a public holiday due to operational necessities, they earn one compensatory day off. This entitlement ensures that employees who sacrifice their holiday time are fairly compensated with equivalent rest time.

The compensatory day off must be taken within 30 days of working the public holiday. Employees should coordinate with their line manager to schedule this day off at a mutually convenient time. Failure to take the compensatory day off within the 30-day window may result in the forfeiture of that specific day, unless extenuating circumstances are approved by HR.

## Applying for Leave

All leave requests must be submitted through the KestrelHR portal. This centralized system ensures accurate recording of leave balances and facilitates approval workflows. Employees are responsible for ensuring that their leave applications are complete and accurate before submission.

### Advance Notice Requirements

For standard annual leave or other planned absences, employees must adhere to specific advance notice periods. Leave longer than 3 days must be applied for at least 14 days in advance. This requirement allows line managers sufficient time to arrange coverage and manage workflow disruptions. Shorter leaves may be approved with less notice, subject to operational requirements and manager discretion.

Emergency leave, such as sudden illness or bereavement, should be reported immediately via phone or email to the line manager, followed by a formal entry in the KestrelHR portal upon return or when able to do so.

## Leave During Probation

New joiners accrue leave from day one of their employment. This means that the entitlement begins building up immediately, regardless of probationary status. However, there are restrictions on the utilization of this accrued time during the initial period of employment.

Employees may only take annual leave after completing 3 months of service. This restriction applies to all permanent shore staff during their probationary period. Sick leave and other statutory leaves remain available as per standard policy from day one. Line managers should be aware of this restriction when approving or denying leave requests for new joiners within their first three months.

## Leave Encashment on Exit

When an employee leaves the company, whether through resignation, retirement, or termination, unused annual leave may be encashed. This provision ensures that employees are compensated for time off they were unable to utilize due to the timing of their departure.

Encashment is calculated based on the basic salary. The payout covers unused annual leave up to a maximum of 30 days. Any balance exceeding this cap will not be paid out and will be forfeited upon exit. Employees are encouraged to use their accrued leave before their last working day to maximize their benefit, subject to approval from their line manager and HRBP.

## Contacts

For any questions regarding this policy, clarification on entitlements, or assistance with leave applications, employees should contact their HR business partner (HRBP). The HRBP is the primary point of contact for all human resources matters and can provide personalized guidance based on individual circumstances.

Contact details for the HRBPs covering the Mumbai and Chennai offices are available on the internal company intranet and via the KestrelHR portal. For urgent matters outside of business hours, employees should follow the emergency contact protocol outlined in the employee handbook.
