# Remote Work and Code of Conduct Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-POL-07
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective Date:** Immediate
**Applicability:** Shore Staff, Kestrel Shipmanagement

## Introduction and Purpose

This policy outlines the operational guidelines for remote work arrangements and establishes the ethical standards expected of all shore staff at Kestrel Shipmanagement. As our organization continues to evolve, we recognize the importance of flexibility in how we work, balanced against the need for accountability, security, and professional integrity. This document serves as the definitive reference for hybrid working arrangements, equipment usage, ethical conduct, and grievance procedures. All employees are required to familiarize themselves with these guidelines to ensure compliance with company standards and local labor regulations.

## Hybrid and Remote Work

Kestrel Shipmanagement supports a flexible work environment designed to enhance productivity and work-life balance while maintaining strong team cohesion. The following rules govern remote work eligibility and scheduling:

- **Eligibility:** Confirmed employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, provided this arrangement is explicitly agreed upon with their manager. This agreement should be documented in the employee’s file or shared calendar to ensure operational visibility.
- **Probationary Period:** Employees currently on probation are required to work from the office exclusively. Remote work privileges are not available during this initial assessment phase to facilitate proper onboarding, mentorship, and cultural integration.
- **Managerial Approval:** Any request for remote work must be discussed and approved by the direct manager prior to implementation. Managers retain the right to deny remote work requests if business continuity or team collaboration requirements cannot be met.

## Working Hours and Availability

To ensure seamless communication across teams and time zones, specific availability windows are mandated for all shore staff, regardless of their physical location.

- **Core Hours:** The designated core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST. During this window, every employee must be reachable via standard company communication channels (email, instant messaging, and phone).
- **Responsiveness:** Employees are expected to respond to urgent inquiries within a reasonable timeframe during core hours. Failure to maintain availability during these specified times may be considered a breach of performance expectations.
- **Flexibility Outside Core Hours:** Work performed outside of core hours is permitted for confirmed employees with prior managerial approval, provided that total working hours do not exceed statutory limits and that rest periods are respected.

## Equipment and Allowances

Kestrel Shipmanagement provides necessary tools to support remote work while ensuring data security and operational efficiency. Financial allowances are also provided to offset the costs associated with working from home.

- **Internet Reimbursement:** To support connectivity requirements, the company provides an internet reimbursement of INR 1,000 per month for remote work. This allowance is processed via payroll and requires employees to maintain valid proof of internet service payments if audited by the finance department.
- **Company-L Provided Equipment:** All necessary hardware, including laptops and peripherals, remains the property of Kestrel Shipmanagement. Employees are responsible for the safekeeping of these items while working remotely.
- **Return of Assets:** Company laptops must be returned within 7 days of the last working day. This applies to both voluntary resignations and terminations. Failure to return assets within this timeframe may result in deductions from final settlements or legal action to recover the equipment.

## Gifts and Hospitality

To maintain objectivity and prevent undue influence, strict guidelines govern the acceptance of gifts and hospitality from external parties.

- **Vendor and Supplier Gifts:** Employees may only accept gifts from vendors or suppliers if the value is no more than INR 2,000 per item. Any gift exceeding this threshold must be declined politely and reported to the compliance team.
- **Recording Requirements:** Every accepted gift, regardless of value, must be recorded in the official gift register. This log includes details such as the giver’s name, date of receipt, description of the item, and estimated value. The register is subject to periodic audit by the internal audit department.
- **Prohibited Items:** Cash, cash equivalents, and gifts intended to influence business decisions are strictly prohibited under all circumstances.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must act in the best interest of Kestrel Shipmanagement at all times. A conflict of interest arises when an employee’s personal interests interfere, or appear to interfere, with their professional duties.

- **Annual Declaration:** A conflict of interest declaration must be submitted every year by 30 April. This declaration requires employees to disclose any external business interests, family relationships with competitors, or other potential conflicts.
- **Ongoing Disclosure:** Employees must also report any new conflicts of interest immediately upon discovery, rather than waiting for the annual submission deadline.
- **Management Review:** All submitted declarations are reviewed by the HR and Compliance departments. Where a conflict is identified, appropriate mitigation measures will be implemented, which may include recusal from specific decisions or reassignment of duties.

## Raising a Grievance

Kestrel Shipmanagement is committed to resolving workplace issues fairly and efficiently. The following procedure outlines the steps for raising and addressing grievances.

- **Initial Step:** Employees should raise their grievance first with the HR business partner assigned to their department. The HR business partner will conduct an initial review and attempt to facilitate a resolution.
- **Escalation Path:** If the issue remains unresolved after discussion with the HR business partner, the case may be escalated to the Grievance Committee. This committee comprises senior management representatives and is empowered to make binding decisions on workplace disputes.
- **Resolution Timeline:** The target is to resolve a grievance within 15 working days from the date of formal submission to the appropriate authority. Employees will be kept informed of the progress throughout the process.

## Prevention of Sexual Harassment

Kestrel Shipmanagement maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward sexual harassment. We are dedicated to providing a safe and respectful work environment for all staff members.

- **Reporting Mechanism:** Complaints regarding sexual harassment must be directed to the Internal Committee (IC). The IC is an independent body established specifically to handle such sensitive matters.
- **Inquiry Process:** Upon receipt of a complaint, the Internal Committee will initiate a formal inquiry. This process ensures confidentiality and impartiality while gathering all necessary facts.
- **Timeline for Resolution:** The Internal Committee must complete its inquiry within 90 days from the date of filing the complaint. Both parties involved will be notified of the findings and any subsequent actions taken.

## Notice Period and Exit

Clear procedures govern the termination of employment to ensure a smooth transition for both the employee and the company.

- **Confirmed Employees:** The notice period for confirmed employees is 60 days. During this period, the employee is expected to continue fulfilling their duties, assist in knowledge transfer, and complete all exit formalities.
- **Probationary Employees:** For employees still on probation, the notice period is 15 days. This shorter duration reflects the preliminary nature of the employment relationship during the initial assessment phase.
- **Exit Formalities:** All employees must complete an exit interview with HR and return all company property, including access cards, laptops, and documents, in accordance with the timelines specified in this policy. Failure to adhere to notice periods may impact final pay, experience letters, or future rehire eligibility.
