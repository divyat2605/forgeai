Write the "Remote Work and Code of Conduct" policy (policy number HR-POL-07, version 1.4) for shore staff of Kestrel Shipmanagement. About 900-1200 words. Sections: Hybrid and remote work, Working hours, Equipment and allowances, Gifts and hospitality, Conflicts of interest, Raising a grievance, Prevention of sexual harassment, Notice period and exit.

FACT SHEET (state every fact exactly):
- Hybrid work: confirmed employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, agreed with their manager. Employees on probation work from the office.
- Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST, when everyone must be reachable.
- Internet reimbursement for remote work: INR 1,000 per month.
- Company laptops must be returned within 7 days of the last working day.
- Gifts from vendors or suppliers: may only be accepted if worth no more than INR 2,000, and every gift must be recorded in the gift register.
- A conflict of interest declaration must be submitted every year by 30 April.
- Grievances: raise first with the HR business partner; unresolved cases go to the Grievance Committee; the target is to resolve a grievance within 15 working days.
- Sexual harassment complaints go to the Internal Committee (IC), which must complete its inquiry within 90 days.
- Notice period: 60 days for confirmed employees; 15 days during probation.
