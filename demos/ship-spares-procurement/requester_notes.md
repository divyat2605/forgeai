# Requester's notes (Purchase Manager, fleet technical department)

Used to answer the interviewer's questions in the recorded demo run. In a live run the requester answers at the terminal instead.

- Users: buyers and technical superintendents in the purchasing team. They know the parts; they need fast, exact answers.
- Answers must quote exact part numbers, prices (USD), stock quantities and lead times from the catalogue - never estimate.
- Order totals: quantity times unit price; mention the supplier's free-delivery threshold or delivery charge when relevant.
- Approval rules and the number of quotations come from the purchasing policy (PUR-POL-02).
- Weather: buyers want the short-term forecast at the delivery port when planning deliveries to a vessel.
- Do not negotiate prices, place orders, or give legal or contract advice; do not discuss suppliers that are not in our documents.
- We have no shipment-tracking system connected yet.
- Tone: concise and factual, bullet points are fine.
