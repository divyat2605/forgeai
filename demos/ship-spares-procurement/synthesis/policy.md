Write the "Fleet Purchasing Policy for Spares and Stores" (PUR-POL-02, version 4) of a ship management company. About 900-1100 words. Sections: Purpose and scope, Requisitions, Approval limits, Quotations, Critical spares, Emergency purchases, Delivery planning, Receiving and inspection, Records.

FACT SHEET (state every fact exactly):
- Requisitions are raised by the Chief Engineer in the purchasing system and reviewed by the Technical Superintendent.
- Approval limits (order value in USD): up to USD 1,000 - Technical Superintendent; USD 1,000 to USD 10,000 - Technical Superintendent and Purchase Manager; above USD 10,000 - Fleet Director.
- Quotations: orders above USD 10,000 need at least 3 competitive quotations; orders between USD 1,000 and USD 10,000 need at least 2.
- Critical spares (marked critical in the catalogue) must never fall below a minimum stock of 2 units on board or in the warehouse.
- Emergency purchases may skip quotations but must be approved by the Fleet Director within 24 hours and documented with a reason.
- Delivery planning: place the order at least the supplier lead time plus 7 days before the vessel's arrival at the delivery port.
- Preferred supplier by region: Europe - SUP-01; South-East Asia and Sri Lanka - SUP-02; Middle East and India - SUP-03.
- Goods must be inspected on receipt and any discrepancy reported to the supplier within their returns window.
- Purchase records are kept for 7 years.
