# Terms of Supply: SUP-02 Straits Ship Chandlers Pte Ltd

## Supplier Profile

This document outlines the commercial terms and conditions governing the supply of ship spare parts by supplier SUP-02, identified as Straits Ship Chandlers Pte Ltd. The supplier is based in Singapore and serves as a primary vendor for our fleet’s maintenance requirements. As a established entity within the maritime logistics sector, Straits Ship Chandlers Pte Ltd provides essential components to ensure operational continuity for vessels under our management. The relationship is defined by strict adherence to the protocols detailed below, ensuring clarity in procurement, logistics, and financial settlement processes.

## Ordering Procedures

To initiate a procurement request, the fleet management team must submit a formal purchase order specifying the part numbers, quantities, and required delivery location. Upon receipt of an order, the supplier commits to a rapid confirmation timeline. Orders are confirmed within 4 working hours. This expedited confirmation process is critical for minimizing downtime in vessel operations, allowing the technical department to plan maintenance windows with certainty.

Once confirmed, the order enters the fulfillment phase. The supplier reserves the right to verify stock availability prior to finalizing the transaction. Any discrepancies between the requested items and available inventory must be communicated to the fleet management team immediately upon discovery to allow for alternative sourcing or schedule adjustments. All correspondence regarding order status should reference the unique purchase order number assigned at the time of confirmation.

## Prices and Payment

Financial transactions are conducted in a standardized currency to mitigate exchange rate volatility. Prices are in USD. This uniformity simplifies accounting procedures across the fleet’s international operations. The standard payment terms are net 30 days from invoice date. Invoices must be submitted electronically to the designated accounts payable department with all necessary customs and shipping documentation attached to facilitate timely processing.

Late payments may result in the suspension of future orders until outstanding balances are cleared. Conversely, early settlement is permitted without penalty. The fleet management team will reconcile invoices against delivery notes and purchase orders prior to approval for payment. Any disputes regarding pricing or invoicing errors must be raised within five business days of invoice receipt. Failure to dispute within this window constitutes acceptance of the charges as accurate.

## Delivery Logistics

The supplier maintains a strategic logistical network to support vessels across key maritime hubs. The designated delivery ports are Singapore, Port Klang and Colombo. For orders placed with a total value above USD 1,500, the supplier provides free delivery to the vessel. This inclusion covers standard shore-to-ship transfer arrangements at berthed locations.

For orders below the threshold of USD 1,500, a delivery charge of USD 90 per order applies. This fee covers the administrative and logistical overhead associated with smaller consignments. Vessels anchored in Singapore require specialized handling due to their location away from the main quay. For these specific cases, launch boat delivery to vessels at anchorage in Singapore costs USD 250 per trip. This cost is non-negotiable and must be factored into the budget for any urgent spare parts required while the vessel is at anchor.

Delivery schedules are subject to port authority regulations and weather conditions. The supplier will provide an estimated time of arrival (ETA) for all deliveries, though this remains an estimate rather than a guaranteed slot unless explicitly stated in writing. It is the responsibility of the vessel’s chief engineer to ensure that the receiving team is available at the specified time to accept the goods.

## Warranty and Returns

All spare parts supplied under this agreement carry a warranty period of 6 months from delivery. This warranty covers manufacturing defects and material failures under normal operating conditions. Claims must be supported by photographic evidence and, where applicable, technical reports from the vessel’s engineering team. The supplier will assess valid claims and provide repair, replacement, or credit as deemed appropriate.

In the event that goods are rejected due to damage upon receipt or non-conformance with specifications, strict return protocols apply. Rejected goods must be returned within 10 days. This timeframe is absolute to ensure inventory accuracy and financial reconciliation. Furthermore, all returned items must be in original packaging. Items returned without their original packaging may be refused by the supplier or subject to restocking fees, as the integrity of the protective materials is essential for preventing further damage during transit back to the supplier’s warehouse.

## Contacts

For operational coordination, the fleet management team should direct all technical queries to the supplier’s logistics department. Commercial disputes and payment inquiries should be addressed to the finance contact provided in the vendor master file. In case of emergency supply needs outside of standard business hours, an after-hours contact number is available via the supplier’s main switchboard. All official communications must be in English to ensure clarity and legal enforceability of the terms outlined herein.
