# Terms of Supply: SUP-03

## Supplier Profile

Gulf Marine Spares LLC operates as the designated supplier under the internal identifier SUP-03. The company is headquartered in Fujairah, United Arab Emirates, serving as the primary logistical hub for our maritime fleet’s maintenance requirements. As a specialized provider of ship spare parts, Gulf Marine Spares LLC maintains a robust inventory tailored to the operational needs of international shipping lines. Their location in Fujairah allows for strategic positioning relative to major maritime routes, facilitating efficient supply chain management for vessels operating in the Middle East and Indian Ocean regions. This partnership is established to ensure continuity of operations, providing critical components that meet strict maritime safety and performance standards. The supplier’s reputation relies on the timely provision of both original equipment manufacturer (OEM) parts and high-quality pattern alternatives, ensuring that fleet downtime is minimized through reliable sourcing capabilities.

## Ordering Procedures

To initiate a procurement cycle with SUP-03, the ship management company must submit detailed requisitions specifying part numbers, quantities, and technical specifications. Orders are processed upon confirmation of availability and acceptance of the provided quotation. It is imperative that all ordering documentation clearly references the supplier ID SUP-03 to ensure proper tracking within our financial systems. The ordering process requires strict adherence to the agreed payment schedule before goods are dispatched. For international shipments, particularly those destined for Mumbai, advance coordination is required to handle regulatory compliance. The supplier will provide necessary commercial invoices and packing lists upon dispatch. Any discrepancies in part specifications must be resolved prior to the release of funds to prevent logistical bottlenecks or installation errors on board vessels.

## Prices and Payment

All financial transactions with Gulf Marine Spares LLC are conducted exclusively in USD. This currency stability simplifies accounting processes for the fleet management team and aligns with international trade practices for marine supplies. The pricing structure is fixed at the time of order confirmation, protecting the buyer from market volatility during the procurement window.

The payment terms are structured to balance cash flow requirements with supplier security. Specifically, the terms require 50% advance payment with the order. This initial deposit secures the inventory and initiates the procurement process. The remaining balance is due within 15 days of delivery. This net-15-day term for the balance allows the ship management company to verify the receipt and condition of goods before final settlement. Invoices must be submitted promptly upon delivery to ensure timely processing of the final payment. Late payments may result in holds on future orders, so financial controllers must monitor the 15-day window closely relative to the delivery date recorded on the bill of lading or airway bill.

## Delivery Logistics

Gulf Marine Spares LLC utilizes a multi-port delivery network to accommodate various vessel itineraries. The designated delivery ports for all shipments are Fujairah, Jebel Ali and Mumbai. These locations were selected to cover key bunkering and maintenance hubs in the region. Shipments to Fujairah and Jebel Ali typically benefit from shorter transit times due to proximity to the supplier’s base. Deliveries to Mumbai require additional logistical planning due to distance and regulatory requirements.

Costs associated with logistics are determined by the total value of the order. Free delivery is provided for orders above USD 3,000. This threshold encourages consolidated purchasing to reduce administrative overhead. For orders below that amount, a delivery charge of USD 150 per order applies. This flat fee covers handling and standard shipping costs regardless of the specific port within the agreed list. Vessels must coordinate with their respective agents at Fujairah, Jebel Ali or Mumbai to arrange offloading upon arrival.

## Warranty Coverage

The quality assurance framework for SUP-03 distinguishes between genuine and non-genuine components. Genuine OEM parts are covered by a warranty of 18 months on genuine OEM parts. This extended period reflects the supplier’s confidence in the durability and performance of original manufacturer components. For alternative solutions, non-OEM (pattern) parts are covered by a warranty of 6 months on non-OEM (pattern) parts. This shorter term accounts for the different manufacturing standards associated with pattern parts. Claims under warranty must be supported by proof of purchase and evidence of improper function under normal operating conditions. The warranty does not cover damage resulting from improper installation, misuse, or lack of routine maintenance by the vessel’s crew.

## Returns Policy

Returns are strictly controlled to prevent inventory fraud and logistical confusion. Returns are accepted only with a returns authorisation number issued within 14 days of delivery. This authorization must be obtained directly from Gulf Marine Spares LLC before any physical return is attempted. The 14-day window begins on the date of delivery as recorded in the shipping documentation. Items returned without this specific number will be refused at the supplier’s facility. All returned goods must be in their original packaging and in resalable condition. The ship management company is responsible for ensuring that the correct authorization code is prominently displayed on all return shipments to facilitate processing.

## Contacts

For operational queries, pricing negotiations, or warranty claims, all communication must be directed to Gulf Marine Spares LLC in Fujairah, United Arab Emirates. Reference supplier ID SUP-03 in all correspondence. Key contact points include the sales department for new orders and the logistics coordinator for delivery tracking. Emergency technical support should be requested via the dedicated marine spares hotline available during standard business hours.
