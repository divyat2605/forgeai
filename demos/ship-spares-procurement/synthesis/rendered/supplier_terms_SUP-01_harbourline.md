# Terms of Supply for Ship Spare Parts

## Supplier Profile

This document outlines the commercial terms and conditions governing the supply of ship spare parts by Harbourline Marine Supplies B.V., identified internally as supplier SUP-01. The supplier is a specialized maritime logistics provider based in Rotterdam, Netherlands. As a key partner in our fleet maintenance operations, Harbourline Marine Supplies B.V. provides critical components required for the operational readiness of our vessels. Their primary hub in Rotterdam allows for efficient coordination with major European shipping lanes, ensuring that technical support and physical supplies are readily available to meet the urgent demands of ship management operations. The relationship is defined by a commitment to reliability, speed, and strict adherence to the procedural frameworks detailed below.

## Ordering Procedures

To ensure timely processing and dispatch of critical spare parts, all procurement activities must adhere to specific communication protocols. Orders are placed exclusively by email to orders@harbourline.example. It is imperative that all purchase requests include precise part numbers, descriptions, quantities, and the required delivery port to facilitate accurate quoting and inventory allocation.

Timing is a critical factor in the supply chain for marine spare parts. The supplier operates on a strict daily dispatch schedule. Orders received by 12:00 CET are dispatched the same working day. This cut-off time is absolute; any orders submitted after this deadline will be processed on the subsequent working day, which may impact vessel maintenance schedules. Consequently, fleet technical managers are advised to submit requisitions early in the day to maximize the likelihood of same-day dispatch.

For situations requiring immediate intervention where standard shipping timelines are insufficient, express logistics options are available. Express (24-hour) delivery is available at a surcharge of 18% of the order value. This premium service should be utilized only for critical failures that threaten vessel safety or regulatory compliance, as it significantly increases procurement costs.

## Prices and Payment

All financial transactions related to the supply of spare parts are conducted in United States Dollars. Prices are in USD. This currency standardization simplifies international accounting and reduces exposure to foreign exchange fluctuations for both parties during the order-to-cash cycle.

The supplier offers a structured delivery charge policy based on the total value of the purchase order. Free delivery to the vessel for orders above USD 2,500 is provided as a standard benefit. This threshold encourages bulk procurement and consolidates shipments, which can be more cost-effective for routine maintenance activities. Conversely, below that a delivery charge of USD 120 per order applies. This flat fee ensures that smaller, urgent orders remain economically viable for the supplier while keeping costs predictable for the ship management company.

Payment terms are set to net 45 days from invoice date. This extended payment window provides the ship management company with substantial cash flow flexibility, allowing time for vessel chartering revenues to be realized before settlement is due. Invoices must be paid in full within this period to maintain good standing and ensure continued priority service levels.

## Delivery Logistics

The supplier utilizes a network of key European ports to facilitate rapid distribution to vessels worldwide. Delivery ports include Rotterdam, Antwerp and Hamburg. These locations serve as the primary transshipment hubs from which parts are forwarded to vessels via sea freight or air cargo, depending on the urgency and destination.

When an order qualifies for free delivery based on its value, the logistics team coordinates directly with vessel agents at the nearest port of call. For orders subject to the standard delivery charge, the cost is added to the invoice, and shipping arrangements follow similar coordination protocols. The choice of departure port may be influenced by vessel itinerary, but the supplier guarantees that all three designated ports are fully stocked for immediate dispatch.

## Warranty and Returns

To protect the ship management company against defective merchandise, a comprehensive warranty policy is in effect. Warranty coverage extends to 12 months from delivery on all parts. This period begins the moment the goods are handed over to the carrier or vessel representative at the point of departure. Any manufacturing defects or failures occurring within this twelve-month window will be addressed through replacement or credit, subject to verification.

In the event of logistical errors, strict reporting timelines apply. Returns: wrong or damaged items must be reported within 7 days of delivery. This short window is necessary to preserve evidence for insurance claims and supplier audits. Reports must include photographic evidence of damage and proof of incorrect item receipt. Failure to report discrepancies within this seven-day period may result in the claim being rejected, placing the financial burden of replacement on the ship management company.

## Contacts

For all administrative, technical, and logistical inquiries related to this supply agreement, designated points of contact must be utilized. Commercial orders and general correspondence should be directed to the dedicated email address: orders@harbourline.example. This channel is monitored by the supplier’s sales team during standard business hours in the Netherlands.

For urgent operational issues that arise outside of standard email response times, such as critical vessel breakdowns requiring immediate logistical intervention, fleet technical managers should reference the emergency contact protocols established in the master vendor agreement. However, all formal documentation, including warranty claims and return authorizations, must still be filed through the primary orders@harbourline.example address to ensure proper tracking and compliance with the terms outlined in this document.
