# Remote Access and VPN Guide

This guide outlines the policies and procedures for accessing the company network remotely. Adherence to these guidelines ensures the security of our data and infrastructure while maintaining productivity for remote workers.

## Who may use VPN

Access to the Virtual Private Network (VPN) is restricted to specific categories of staff to maintain security integrity. The VPN client, GlobalConnect, is available to all permanent employees. Additionally, contractors are eligible for access provided they have submitted a manager-approved request. This approval process ensures that only authorized personnel with a legitimate business need can connect to internal resources.

## Connecting

To establish a secure connection, users must install the GlobalConnect client on their device. Once installed, log in using your standard corporate credentials. It is important to note that VPN sessions are time-limited for security reasons. Sessions automatically disconnect after 12 hours. When this occurs, you must re-establish the connection by logging out and logging back in via the GlobalConnect interface. Do not attempt to bypass this timeout mechanism, as it is a critical security feature designed to limit exposure windows.

## Split tunnelling

For security reasons, split tunnelling is disabled on all company devices. This means that all network traffic generated by your device while connected to the VPN will be routed through the encrypted tunnel back to the corporate network. You cannot configure the client to allow local internet traffic to bypass the VPN while accessing internal resources. This policy ensures that no data leaks outside the secure channel and prevents potential vulnerabilities associated with mixed routing paths. Users should expect all web browsing and application traffic to appear as if it originates from the office network while the VPN is active.

## Public Wi-Fi

Using public Wi-Fi networks poses significant security risks, including man-in-the-middle attacks and data interception. Therefore, you must never use public Wi-Fi without the VPN active. If you are working from a coffee shop, airport, or hotel, ensure that GlobalConnect is connected and showing a healthy status before accessing any company resources or sensitive data. Connecting to unsecured networks without encryption is strictly prohibited. If your VPN connection drops while on public Wi-Fi, immediately disconnect from the public network until you can re-establish the secure tunnel.

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues connecting to the VPN, follow these steps to resolve common problems. The majority of connection errors can be fixed by restarting the GlobalConnect client. Close the application completely and reopen it to refresh the connection state. If restarting the client does not resolve the issue, check your local network configuration. Specifically, if you receive error code 809, this indicates that your home router is blocking the VPN port. In this case, you will need to contact your IT support team to assist with router configuration or consider using a different network connection where the port is not blocked.
