# Company Devices and Software Request Policy

This policy outlines the guidelines for the issuance, maintenance, and security of company-provided hardware and software. Adherence to these procedures ensures operational continuity, data security, and equitable resource management across the organization. All employees are expected to comply with these standards to maintain a secure and efficient working environment.

## Laptops

The provision of laptop computers is central to our remote and hybrid work capabilities. To ensure performance reliability and security compliance, we adhere to a strict lifecycle management program for all hardware assets.

- The standard laptop refresh cycle is every 4 years. This timeline balances the need for up-to-date technology with fiscal responsibility.
- Employees are not permitted to voluntarily upgrade their devices before this scheduled interval unless explicitly authorized due to specific technical requirements or role changes.
- Upon completion of the four-year period, IT will initiate the replacement process. The old device must be returned in good working condition, including all original accessories and power supplies.
- Data migration support is available during the transition period. Employees should back up personal files to approved cloud storage solutions prior to handing over their device.

## Mobile Phones

Mobile connectivity is a critical component of our business operations, but issuance is strictly controlled to manage costs and security risks. Access to company-funded mobile devices is not universal.

- Company phones are issued only to staff on the on-call rota and to managers. This restriction ensures that devices are allocated to roles where immediate accessibility is a operational necessity.
- Staff members who do not meet these criteria must use their personal devices for work-related communications, adhering to the acceptable use guidelines outlined in the BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) policy.
- Managers wishing to request a device for an on-call team member must submit a formal justification through the IT portal, detailing the specific operational need.
- All issued phones are subject to remote wipe capabilities and mandatory encryption protocols. Loss or theft of these devices poses a significant security risk and is treated with high priority.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

The security of company data is paramount. In the event that any company-issued hardware is lost, stolen, or compromised, immediate action is required to mitigate potential data breaches.

- Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours to the Service Desk. Delayed reporting increases the risk of unauthorized access to sensitive corporate information.
- Reports should include the date and time of the incident, the location where the device was last seen, and any relevant details regarding the circumstances of the loss.
- The Service Desk will immediately initiate remote lock and wipe procedures to protect data integrity.
- Employees are required to file a police report for stolen items if applicable. A copy of this report must be submitted to the IT Security team within 48 hours of the incident.
- Failure to report a lost or stolen device within the specified timeframe may result in disciplinary action and potential financial liability for the employee.

## Software Requests

To maintain system stability and security, all software installations on company devices are centralized and controlled. Unauthorized installations can introduce vulnerabilities and compatibility issues.

- All software requests go through the IT portal. This centralized system allows for tracking, auditing, and approval workflows.
- Requests require manager approval before they are processed by the IT team. Managers are responsible for verifying that the requested software is necessary for the employee’s role.
- Standard software is installed within 3 working days of approval. This timeline applies to commonly used applications such as office suites, communication tools, and standard industry-specific software.
- Requests for non-standard or specialized software may take longer due to additional security reviews and licensing checks. Employees should plan ahead and submit requests well in advance of project deadlines.

## Personal Use

While company devices are provided to facilitate work, they remain the property of the organization and must be used primarily for business purposes.

- Personal software may not be installed on company laptops. This rule prevents security vulnerabilities, licensing violations, and performance degradation caused by unvetted applications.
- Employees are permitted to use personal email accounts or cloud storage services for limited personal tasks, provided this does not interfere with work duties or compromise data security.
- Monitoring of network traffic may occur to ensure compliance with this policy. Any attempt to bypass security controls or install unauthorized software will be investigated and may result in disciplinary action.
