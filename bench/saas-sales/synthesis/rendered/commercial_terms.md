# Commercial Terms of Service for FlowDesk

## Billing

All pricing for the FlowDesk platform is structured on a per-user, per-month basis. The currency used for all transactions and invoices is United States Dollars (USD). When reviewing the official price list, customers should note that these figures represent the standard monthly rate applicable to each individual user account licensed under the service agreement.

Invoices are generated automatically based on the number of active users at the time of billing. It is the responsibility of the account administrator to ensure that the user count accurately reflects the current workforce authorized to access the system. Any discrepancies in user counts must be reported and adjusted prior to the next billing cycle to avoid overpayment or service interruption due to non-payment.

For organizations requiring custom volume discounts or enterprise-level licensing structures, direct negotiation with the FlowDesk sales team is required outside of the standard public price list. However, all standard commercial terms regarding payment methods, tax liabilities, and invoice delivery remain consistent across all tiers unless explicitly stated in a separate master service agreement.

## Annual Plans

FlowDesk offers an annual subscription model designed for customers seeking long-term stability and cost efficiency. By opting for annual prepayment, subscribers receive a significant financial advantage compared to the month-to-month standard rate. Specifically, annual prepayment grants a 15% discount on the monthly price. This discount is applied to the base rate found in the current price list and is locked in for the duration of the annual term.

To secure this discounted rate, payment must be made in full at the beginning of the contract term. Partial payments or installment plans are not available for the annual tier; the entire annual fee must be settled upfront. This prepayment structure allows FlowDesk to offer reduced rates while maintaining predictable revenue streams.

Upon renewal of an annual plan, the standard terms of service apply unless a new agreement is signed. Customers wishing to maintain their discounted rate must ensure timely payment before the expiration of the current term. Failure to renew on time may result in the account reverting to monthly billing rates for any subsequent usage until the next annual cycle is established.

## Refunds

FlowDesk maintains a clear and strict refund policy to protect both the customer and the integrity of the service ecosystem. For new customers, a full refund is available within 30 days of the first purchase. This window allows organizations to evaluate the platform’s fit for their specific workflows without financial risk during the initial trial period. To qualify for this refund, the request must be submitted within the thirty-day window from the date of the original transaction.

It is critical to note that no refunds are issued for renewals. Once an annual term has commenced or a monthly cycle has been completed and renewed, the payment is considered final. This policy applies to all tiers and plans, including those with discounted annual rates. Customers should carefully consider their commitment level before entering into any renewal agreement, as post-renewal funds are non-refundable under any circumstances.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)

FlowDesk is committed to providing a reliable and high-performance environment for its users. The platform guarantees a monthly uptime of 99.9%. This metric is calculated based on the total number of minutes in a calendar month minus any downtime experienced by the service. Downtime is defined as any period where the core functionality of FlowDesk is unavailable to users due to technical failures on FlowDesk’s side, excluding scheduled maintenance windows communicated in advance.

If FlowDesk fails to meet the 99.9% monthly uptime target, eligible customers may receive service credits. The credit structure is calculated as follows: for each full 0.1% that the actual uptime falls below the target, the customer receives a service credit equal to 10% of the monthly fee. This calculation ensures that compensation scales with the severity of the outage.

However, there is a cap on these credits. The total service credits awarded for any single month cannot exceed 50% of the monthly fee. Customers must submit a claim within thirty days of the incident to be eligible for these credits. Credits are applied to future invoices and do not result in cash refunds.

## Support

FlowDesk provides dedicated assistance channels to ensure operational continuity for its customers. For urgent issues requiring immediate attention, 24/7 phone support is available. The service level commitment for this channel ensures that calls are answered within 1 hour of initiation. This rapid response time is designed to minimize disruption during critical business hours or unexpected technical emergencies.

For non-urgent inquiries, documentation requests, or feature questions, email support is the primary channel. FlowDesk commits to responding to all email support tickets within 1 business day. Responses are typically provided by qualified support engineers who can address technical configurations, billing questions, and general usage guidance.

Both support channels require valid account credentials for access. Customers are encouraged to provide detailed descriptions of issues, including error logs and steps to reproduce problems, to facilitate faster resolution times. Support availability is subject to the terms of the active subscription; lapsed accounts may experience delayed or restricted access to these channels.

## Data Retention

Data integrity and privacy are paramount in the FlowDesk architecture. When a customer cancels their subscription, their account enters a grace period during which their data is preserved. Specifically, customer data is kept for 90 days after cancellation. This retention period allows organizations time to export their information, migrate to alternative platforms, or reactivate their accounts if necessary.

After the expiration of this 90-day window, all customer data is permanently deleted from FlowDesk’s servers and backups. This deletion process is irreversible. Customers are strongly advised to utilize the 90-day period to back up any critical records, reports, or configurations they wish to retain. Once the deletion occurs, no recovery is possible, and FlowDesk assumes no liability for lost data beyond this timeframe.
