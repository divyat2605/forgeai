Write a ship SMS procedure for enclosed space entry, ~700 words, sections: Scope, Permit to enter, Atmosphere testing, Attendant and communication, Rescue, Records.
FACT SHEET:
- Entry needs a permit signed by the Master; the permit is valid for one shift of maximum 8 hours.
- Acceptable atmosphere: oxygen 20.8% by volume, hydrocarbons below 1% LFL, H2S below 5 ppm, CO below 25 ppm.
- The space must be ventilated for at least 30 minutes before testing.
- An attendant must remain at the entrance at all times; communication checks every 10 minutes.
- Enclosed space rescue drills are held every 2 months.
- Permits are kept on board for 3 years.
