Write a ship Safety Management System procedure for bunkering (fuel transfer), ~700 words, sections: Purpose, Responsibilities, Pre-bunkering meeting, Checklist, During transfer, Completion, Spill response.
FACT SHEET:
- The Chief Engineer is the person in charge of bunkering.
- A pre-bunkering safety meeting with the barge crew must be held and the checklist signed by both parties.
- Tanks must not be filled above 90% of capacity.
- Transfer starts at a reduced rate for the first 10 minutes; maximum pumping pressure 5 bar unless agreed otherwise.
- Bunkering must be stopped if wind speed exceeds 30 knots or during thunderstorms.
- Scuppers must be plugged and the SOPEP locker opened before starting.
