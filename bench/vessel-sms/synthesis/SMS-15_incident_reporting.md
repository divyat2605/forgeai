Write a ship SMS procedure on incident and near-miss reporting, ~600 words, sections: What to report, Immediate actions, Reporting timelines, Investigation, Lessons learned.
FACT SHEET:
- All injuries, spills, near misses and equipment failures affecting safety must be reported.
- Serious incidents are reported to the Designated Person Ashore (DPA) by phone immediately and in writing within 24 hours.
- Near-miss reports are submitted within 72 hours in the reporting system.
- Investigations of serious incidents start within 48 hours.
- Each vessel must submit at least 2 near-miss reports per month.
