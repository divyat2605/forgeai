Write a handbook page on attendance, coursework deadlines and extensions, ~600 words, sections: Attendance, Submitting coursework, Late submission, Extensions, Mitigating circumstances.
FACT SHEET:
- Minimum attendance is 80% of timetabled sessions.
- Coursework is submitted online by 14:00 on the deadline day.

- Mitigating circumstances claims must be made within 5 working days of the assessment.
