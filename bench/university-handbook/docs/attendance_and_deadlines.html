Attendance and Coursework Policy Handbook

Attendance

Regular participation in scheduled academic activities is fundamental to your success and engagement with the curriculum. The institution maintains a strict expectation regarding presence during the term. You are required to maintain a minimum attendance rate of 80% of timetabled sessions. This threshold ensures that you receive the full benefit of lectures, seminars, and practical workshops, which are designed to complement independent study.

Attendance is monitored through automated registration systems and manual roll calls conducted by faculty members. If your attendance falls below the required percentage, you may be flagged for academic review. Persistent absence without valid justification can result in formal warnings or intervention from the student support team. It is your responsibility to check your timetabled sessions regularly and ensure you are present unless you have an approved excuse.

Submitting Coursework

All coursework assignments must be submitted electronically via the designated online learning platform. The submission window closes precisely at 14:00 on the deadline day. Submissions made after this time will not be accepted as on-time, regardless of technical issues or internet connectivity problems. It is strongly advised to upload your work well in advance of the final minute to avoid last-minute system errors.

Files should be uploaded in the specified format, typically PDF or Word documents, unless otherwise stated in the module handbook. Each submission must include your student ID and the assignment title as indicated in the brief. Once submitted, you will receive an automated confirmation email; please retain this for your records. If you do not receive a confirmation within one hour of uploading, contact the IT helpdesk immediately.

Late Submission

Failure to submit coursework by the 14:00 deadline on the due date will result in a penalty applied to your final mark. For work submitted up to five days late, a deduction of 5 percentage points per day is applied. This means that if you submit one day late, 5 points are removed; two days late results in a 10-point deduction, and so on.

This penalty structure applies cumulatively for each calendar day the submission is overdue. However, once coursework is submitted more than 5 days after the original deadline, it will receive a mark of zero. Work submitted beyond this five-day window is not marked and does not count toward your final grade. There are no exceptions to this rule unless you have an approved extension or mitigating circumstances claim that has been processed prior to the assessment date.

Extensions

In certain situations, you may be eligible for a formal extension if you anticipate being unable to meet a deadline due to manageable but unforeseen circumstances. Extensions of up to 7 days can be requested before the deadline through the student portal. You must submit this request prior to the original submission time; retroactive extensions are generally not granted unless supported by strong mitigating evidence.

To apply for an extension, you must provide relevant evidence that supports your request. This could include documentation from a medical professional, employer, or other authoritative source. The student portal will guide you through the necessary steps to upload this evidence and specify the new deadline. Approval is subject to review by the module leader and academic support staff. If approved, your new deadline will be clearly stated in the portal confirmation.

Mitigating Circumstances

If you experience significant personal issues that affect your ability to study or submit work on time, you may submit a mitigating circumstances claim. These claims are intended for serious events such as illness, bereavement, or other profound personal disruptions. Claims must be made within 5 working days of the assessment date or deadline.

You must provide credible evidence to support your claim, such as medical certificates or official letters. The academic board will review each case individually. If approved, you may be offered a resit opportunity without penalty or an extension, depending on the nature of the circumstances and institutional policy. It is crucial to act promptly and submit all required documentation within the specified timeframe to ensure your claim is considered valid.
